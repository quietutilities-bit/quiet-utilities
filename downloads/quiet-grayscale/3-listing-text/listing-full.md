## Description (Chrome and Firefox — paste as is)

Color is how websites grab your attention. Quiet Grayscale turns it down.

Switch a site to black and white and the red badges, flashy thumbnails and bright buttons lose their pull. Many people find it makes endless scrolling less tempting and long reading easier on the eyes.

HOW IT WORKS
• Grayscale this site: one switch in the popup. It remembers the site every time you come back.
• Every website: go gray everywhere, and skip the sites you want in color.
• Strength slider: from a light fade to full black and white.
• Alt+Shift+G: gray the tab you're on for now, or pause a gray site for a moment. Change the shortcut any time in your browser's shortcut settings.
• Light and dark popup that follows your system.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works entirely in your browser.
• No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
• Asks for access only to the sites you choose. "Every website" asks for all sites only when you turn it on, and you can take that back any time.

Free, with no upsells. Made by Quiet Utilities, makers of small browser tools that do one job well.

## Single purpose (Chrome privacy tab)

Quiet Grayscale shows websites in grayscale (black and white) on the sites the user picks, or on every site, to reduce visual distraction.

## Permission justifications (Chrome privacy tab)

- **storage:** Saves the user's choices: which sites are gray, the every-website setting, skipped sites and the strength level. Stored locally in the browser.
- **activeTab:** Lets the popup and the keyboard shortcut gray the current tab right away when the user clicks or presses the shortcut, without needing access to every site.
- **scripting:** Adds one CSS filter rule to pages the user has chosen to show in grayscale, and registers that rule for those sites so it applies on future visits.
- **Host permissions (optional, requested at runtime):** When the user switches on "Grayscale this site", the extension asks for access to that one site only, so it can keep the site gray on later visits. "<all_urls>" is requested only if the user turns on "Every website". Both can be revoked at any time.
- **Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. All code is packaged in the extension.

## Notes for certification / reviewers

Quiet Grayscale is free and has no account, payment or network requests. To test: open any normal website, click the toolbar icon and switch on "Grayscale this site" (the browser asks for access to that site only). The page turns black and white and stays that way after reload. "Every website" requests access to all sites and grays every page; individual sites can then be skipped. The Strength slider changes the intensity. Alt+Shift+G grays the current tab temporarily. Browser pages and store pages can't be changed, and the popup says so.
