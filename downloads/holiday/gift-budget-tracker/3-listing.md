## Description
Know exactly what you've spent on Christmas, and what's left, without doing a single sum. This Christmas Gift & Budget Tracker is a ready-made spreadsheet for Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel: type names, budgets and prices, and the totals, per-person budgets and progress bars update by themselves.

WHAT'S INSIDE (7 tabs)
- Dashboard: total budget, spent, left to spend, budget used, gifts bought / wrapped / given, days until Christmas, savings progress and spending by category
- Budget: holiday budget by category (gifts, food and treats, decorations, wrapping, cards and postage, parties, travel, outfits, charity, kids' activities, other) with spent, left and % used
- People: gift budget by person (up to 50 people) with planned, spent, left and a status that updates itself (Shopping, To wrap, Ready to give, All given, Over budget, Plan over budget)
- Gift Tracker: up to 300 gifts with a person dropdown, store or link, price and Bought / Wrapped / Given dropdowns
- Expense Log: everything that isn't a gift, with categories and a running total
- Savings: set a goal and a start date; it works out how much to save each week and shows if you're on track
- Start Here: set-up in 5 steps

YOU GET 2 FILES
- Christmas-Gift-Budget-Tracker.xlsx: the blank tracker, ready for your list
- Christmas-Gift-Budget-Tracker-Example.xlsx: the same tracker filled in with sample data, so you can see how it works
