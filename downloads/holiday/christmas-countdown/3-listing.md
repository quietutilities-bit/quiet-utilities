## Description
Count down to Christmas with one simple, happy activity every day from December 1 to Christmas Eve. Print the cards, tuck one into an envelope, bag or jar for each day, and let your kids color the countdown chart as the big day gets closer.

Most activities use things you already have at home: paper snowflakes, a cocoa night, a blanket fort, a dance party, a lights walk, kindness surprises and more. Some cards come with their own page in the pack (a coloring page, a maze, a counting page, a wish list or kindness coupons), and the card tells you which page to print.

WHAT'S INSIDE (23 pages)
- 24 activity cards (6 per page), one for each day, December 1 to 24
- Countdown Tree chart: color one numbered ornament each day
- Countdown Calendar chart: the suggested order with a star to color each day
- 6 blank cards to add your own family traditions
- 24 round number tags for envelopes, bags or jars
- 6 hand-drawn coloring pages: Christmas Lights, Winter Owl, Sled Full of Presents, Candy Jar, Cozy Bunny, Ornament Trio
- Decorate Your Tree drawing page
- Reindeer Maze and Count It Up counting page (answers included)
- My Christmas Wish List
- 8 Kindness Coupons to cut out and give
- Countdown Champion certificate for Christmas Eve
- Start-here guide for grown-ups

Best for ages 3 to 10. Little ones will need a grown-up to read the cards and help with scissors.

SIZES
- US Letter (8.5 x 11 in)
- A4 (210 x 297 mm)

HOW TO USE
1. Download the PDFs from your Etsy Purchases page (or the link in your confirmation email).
2. Print at home at "Actual size" / "100%". Card stock makes the cards and tags sturdier.
3. Cut the cards apart and open one each day from December 1. Print the pages again as often as you like for your own family.
