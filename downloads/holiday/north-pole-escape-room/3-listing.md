## Description
The sleigh won't launch! A snowstorm scrambled the launch computer, and only your family can find the 10-letter launch code before Christmas Eve. The North Pole Escape Room is a printable puzzle game for families and groups, ages 8 and up. Plan on 60 to 90 minutes of teamwork, laughing and "wait, I've got it!"

HOW IT WORKS
Hide 10 puzzle envelopes around the house. Each puzzle reveals one letter of the sleigh's launch code. When the players have all 10 letters, they say the code and open the "sleigh" (a box with a treat or a gift you choose).

WHAT'S INSIDE (22 pages)
- 10 puzzles: picture math, a secret radio code, a forest maze, word scrambles, a "who opened the gate?" logic grid, spot the difference, a picture grid puzzle, number patterns, a word search with a hidden message and a mirror-writing window
- Story cards to read aloud (start and finale)
- Launch Panel code sheet with puzzle checklist
- 2-page host guide: set-up in about 20 minutes, plus tips to make it easier or harder
- 10 hint cards and 12 envelope labels
- 6 elf crew badges and a "Sleigh Launch Control Room" door sign
- Full answer key
- "Sleigh Launched!" certificate

GOOD TO KNOW
- For 2 to 10 players: one big team, or print two sets and race two teams
- Ages 8+; younger kids can play with an older helper
- Solve puzzles in any order
- You'll need 12 envelopes, pencils, scissors and a small treat (a mirror is handy for one puzzle)
- Color printing is best
