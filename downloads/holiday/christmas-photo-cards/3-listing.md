## Description
Make your own family Christmas photo cards in minutes. You get 3 ready-made designs that open in Canva: drag in your photo, type your names and the year, then print at home, order prints, or send them by text and email. A free Canva account is all you need.

THE 3 DESIGNS
- Merry & Bright: portrait 5 x 7 in, cream with holly and gold sparkles
- Joy to the World: portrait 5 x 7 in, deep green with gold and snowflakes
- Season's Greetings: landscape 7 x 5 in, a wide photo plus a little Christmas tree

WHAT YOU GET
- A START HERE PDF (US Letter and A4 versions, same content) with your 3 Canva template links
- A step-by-step guide: add your photo, change the words, download and print
- Printing tips for home printers, photo shops and pharmacies, plus a short FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download the PDF from your Etsy Purchases page and click a template link.
2. Log in to Canva (free) and click "Use template". Canva makes your own copy.
3. Drag your photo onto the photo box; it snaps in. Double-click to adjust.
4. Double-click the text to change the greeting, family name and year. Colors and fonts can be changed too.
5. Download a print-ready PDF or JPG. Print at home on cardstock, order 5 x 7 cards from any photo shop, or share online.
