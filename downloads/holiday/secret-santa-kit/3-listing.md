## Description
Run a fun, fair Secret Santa gift exchange without the group-chat chaos. This fillable Secret Santa Kit has everything you need: invitations, a rules poster, wish lists, name-draw slips, an organizer's tracker, gift tags and a guess-who game for the big reveal. Works for families, friends, classrooms and the office, for 4 to 24 people.

WHAT'S INSIDE (11 pages)
- Organizer guide: run it in 5 steps, a simple timeline, and how to draw names when you can't meet in person
- Invitations: 2 per page; fill in who, date, time, place, budget, name-draw date and RSVP
- Rules poster: your group name, budget, wish-list deadline and exchange date + 8 friendly rules
- Wish list, full page: favorite things, sizes, three wishes, "please no" (allergies, dislikes) and little things that make my day
- Mini wish lists: 2 per page, a quick version
- 24 name-draw slips (2 pages): type or write the names, cut, fold and draw from a hat
- Organizer's tracker: who has whom for up to 20 people, with wish-list and gift check boxes
- 12 gift tags: "To: ___ From: your Secret Santa"
- Guess your Secret Santa: 6 game cards for the reveal
- Thank-you notes: 4 per page

SIZES
- US Letter (8.5 x 11 in)
- A4 (210 x 297 mm)
You get both fillable PDFs.

HOW TO USE
1. Download the PDFs from your Etsy Purchases page (or the link in your confirmation email).
2. Open in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader (computer, phone or tablet); Chrome, Edge and Mac Preview work too. Click a box and type.
3. Save and print at "Actual size" / "100%", or send the filled-in PDF to your group. Print as many copies as your group needs.
Prefer a pen? Every page also prints blank to fill in by hand.
