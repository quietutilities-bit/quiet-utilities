## Description
Gus the turkey has locked the pantry, and nobody gets dessert until the kids crack his secret 8-letter code! Turkey Trouble is a printable escape room for kids ages 7 to 12. It keeps them busy (and thinking) for 45 to 60 minutes while the grown-ups get dinner on the table.

HOW IT WORKS
Hide 8 puzzle envelopes around the house. Each puzzle reveals one letter of the pantry code. When the kids have all 8 letters, they say the code word and win the treat you've hidden in the "pantry".

WHAT'S INSIDE (17 pages)
- 8 puzzles: pie math, a picture-code message, a corn maze, word scrambles, a picture logic grid, spot the difference, a "who brought the pie?" detective puzzle and number patterns
- Story cards to read aloud (start and finale)
- Pantry Lock code sheet with puzzle checklist
- 2-page host guide: set-up in about 15 minutes, plus tips to make it easier or harder
- 8 hint cards and 10 envelope labels
- Full answer key
- "Pantry Unlocked!" certificate

GOOD TO KNOW
- For 1 to 8 players: solo, in teams, or kids with an older helper
- Solve puzzles in any order
- You'll need 9 envelopes, pencils, scissors and a small treat
- Color printing is best (one puzzle uses colored leaves)
