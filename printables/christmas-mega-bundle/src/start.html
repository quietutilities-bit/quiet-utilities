Good to know

  • Opening a zip: on a computer, double-click it (Windows: right-click, "Extract All"). On iPhone, tap it in the Files app; on Android, open it in Files by Google.
  • Printing: print at "Actual size" or "100%", not "Fit". Card stock is best for cards, tags and badges; color printing is best for the escape room.
  • Print only what you need. You don't have to print every page; pick the games and pages you want.
  • Fillable PDFs (Letter from Santa, Secret Santa): open them in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader to type in the boxes, then print. You can also print them blank and write by hand.
  • Spreadsheet: in Google Sheets, open sheets.google.com and choose File > Import > Upload; in Excel, just open it. Then follow the Start Here tab.
  • For personal use only. Please don't share or resell the files.
